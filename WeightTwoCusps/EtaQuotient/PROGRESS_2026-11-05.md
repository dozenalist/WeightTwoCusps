## Current state

`etaQuotientMF` and `etaQuotientCuspForm` are the public constructions from
the finite Newman–Ligozat conditions. They allow odd and negative exponents
at every positive level. The cusp-form constructor now reuses the modular
form's slash-invariant function and holomorphy proof.

The underlying function is `etaQuotient N r`, with `r : Fin N → ℤ` and entry
`i` assigned to `η((i.val + 1)z)`. Nondivisor entries are ignored. Omitted
constructor proofs use `decide +kernel`; explicit symbolic proofs remain accepted.

## Coefficient mathematics completed

For arbitrary signed exponents, assume `24 ∣ infinitySum N r` and
`0 ≤ infinitySum N r`. No modularity theorem is needed.

- `qExpansion_coeff_eq_coefficient` gives a finite integer formula for every
  actual analytic q-expansion coefficient.
- The formula uses Euler coefficients, finite Cauchy products, natural powers
  and a reciprocal recurrence with constant coefficient one. Its correctness
  is proved using formal power series and analytic q-expansion uniqueness.
- Exposed Euler arithmetic and `divisorList` avoid reliance on opaque
  Mathlib bodies in downstream numerical certificates. `eulerCoefficient_cast`
  and the divisor-list membership theorem prove agreement with Mathlib's APIs.
- `qExpansion_coeff_add` shifts by the leading infinity order;
  `qExpansion_coeff_integral`, `_re`, and `_im` prove integrality and reality.
- `qExpansion_coeff_leading_add_two` gives the symbolic coefficient
  `r₁(r₁ - 3)/2 - r₂`, counting `r₂` only when 2 divides the level.
  Integer versions are `two_mul_unitCoefficient_two` and `unitCoefficient_two`.
- `qExpansion_coeff_mul_of_exponent_add` identifies multiplication with finite
  integer convolution. `qExpansion_ne_zero` proves nonvanishing of the series.
- `qExpansion_eq_mk_coefficient` and `hasSum_coefficient` identify the entire
  convergent expansion with the integer algorithm throughout the upper half-plane.
- Constructor-specific coefficient, order and leading-coefficient lemmas
  discharge the arithmetic hypotheses from `NewmanLigozatConditions`.
  A constructed cusp form has positive infinity order and constant coefficient zero.

The existing leading coefficient, first following coefficient, infinity order,
and local cusp-order results remain available under the new module paths.

## Numeral tactic completed

`WeightTwoCusps/Tactic/EtaCoefficient.lean` provides `eta_coeff` and registers
three cooperating simp procedures for coefficients and constant coefficients.
Both `by simp` and `by eta_coeff` evaluate numeral levels and indices with
closed integer exponent functions, including both bundled constructors.

Intermediate tables are proposed by metaprograms and checked by finite
kernel reduction. Table correctness lemmas propagate the certificates through
reciprocals, powers and products. Zero exponent factors avoid unnecessary
Euler-table checks. Native computation supplies candidates, never proof evidence.
