## Available tactics

`import WeightTwoCusps.Tactic` loads the dimension and eta-coefficient
procedures; the main `WeightTwoCusps` import exports them too.

| Module | Evaluation |
| --- | --- |
| `Gamma_0.lean` | `gamma0Dimension` and weight-two `Γ₀` finrank |
| `Gamma_1.lean` | `gamma1Dimension` and weight-two `Γ₁` finrank |
| `Gamma.lean` | `gammaDimension` and weight-two principal-group finrank |
| `EtaCoefficient.lean` | Signed eta-quotient coefficients by `simp` or `eta_coeff` |

The subgroup modules can be imported individually. Shared dimension arithmetic
and certificate helpers remain in `Basic.lean`.

```lean
import WeightTwoCusps

open WeightTwoCusps UpperHalfPlane

example : gamma0Dimension 11 = 1 := by dsimp
example : Module.finrank ℂ (gamma0CuspForms 11) = 1 := by simp
example : (qExpansion 1 (etaQuotient 1 ![24])).coeff 10 = -115920 := by eta_coeff
example : (qExpansion 1 (etaQuotient 4 ![-8, 20, 0, -8])).coeff 2 = 24 := by simp
```

## Eta-coefficient implementation

`eta_coeff` uses the proved full integer coefficient formula in
`EtaQuotient/QExpansion/`. It computes finite intermediate tables and checks
each table by kernel reduction. Correctness propagates through convolution,
integer powers, reciprocal recursion and the infinity-order shift.

The registered procedures are:

- `WeightTwoCusps.Tactic.etaQuotientCoeff_ofNat`, before ordinary simplification;
- `WeightTwoCusps.Tactic.etaQuotientCoeff_post`, after constructor coercions simplify;
- `WeightTwoCusps.Tactic.etaQuotientConstantCoeff_ofNat`, for coefficient zero
  after it becomes `PowerSeries.constantCoeff`.

They accept numeral levels and indices, vector literals and closed exponent
definitions, including negative individual exponents. Both `etaQuotientMF`
and `etaQuotientCuspForm` are supported. Nondivisor entries are ignored.

Numerical candidates are never trusted as proofs. No new axiom,
`native_decide`, `sorry`, or unsafe proof mechanism is used.

## Scope and remaining improvements

- Eta evaluation uses period one and requires a nonnegative infinity sum
  divisible by 24. These conditions follow from the modular-form constructor
  hypotheses. Unsupported infinity orders and symbolic data are left alone.
- Additional degree-100 odd/signed benchmarks were stopped without a result;
  they are not included in the verified regression or benchmark suite.
- Faster batch computation and smaller high-precision certificates remain
  possible improvements; larger calculations may require more resources.
- Dimension arithmetic supports numeral levels including zero. Analytic
  finrank evaluation requires positive levels and weight two.
- `dsimp` works for the finite arithmetic definition `gamma0Dimension`.
  Analytic finrank and the abstract indices in the other dimension formulas
  require proof-producing `simp`, since their evaluations are not definitional.

There are no known unfinished proof obligations for the implemented cases.
